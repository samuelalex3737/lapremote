Welcome. Let's pair your phone.

  1. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as this laptop.
  2. Open your phone's camera and scan the QR code below.
  3. If Windows asks whether to allow LapRemote on the network, choose Private networks only.
  4. On the phone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen to get an app icon.
  5. Optional extras: the AI assistant (docs/AI.md) and HTTPS for voice and the air mouse (docs/HTTPS.md).
